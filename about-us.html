Our Story

Kitsune

Founded by Matt Kusain currently taking BSIT-MWA in National University Manila, Kitsune was born from a simple dream: to create PC hardware that feels as alive as the spirits of legend. Our name, Kitsune (キツネ), represents the intelligence, power, and shapeshifting beauty we build into every component.

Design Philosophy

We believe your PC is an extension of your soul. Our design philosophy centers on "Functional Aesthetic." We don't just add a coat of paint; we engineer our Sakura, Kawaii Neko, and Fox series from the ground up to ensure that world-class cooling and performance are never sacrificed for beauty.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower gamers, creators, and enthusiasts to build rigs that break the mold. We strive to provide a complete ecosystem of hardware that reflects the vibrant, magical energy of anime culture while delivering the raw power needed to conquer any digital realm.
